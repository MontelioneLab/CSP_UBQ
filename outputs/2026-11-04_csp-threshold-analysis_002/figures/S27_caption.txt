SI Figure S27. CSP threshold sensitivity and intensity distributions for 135 pH/temperature-matched apo/holo targets (12,267 eligible residues). Binding-site labels use the fixed canonical structural union. Residues without finite CSPs, structural labels or saved baseline significance masks, prolines, and CSP z > 100 are excluded. A: Individual target ROC curves (gray), pooled ROC from CSP/primary cutoff (blue), and equal-target mean ROC (dashed). Mean AUC is the arithmetic mean of target AUCs; the curve is interpolated. B: Pooled distal fraction and sensitivity as each frozen target cutoff is multiplied by m. Stars in A/B use the original saved primary significance flags. C: Residue-level TP/FP CSP distributions at the saved primary rule. D: Per-target median TP/FP CSP distributions with paired lines for 134 targets; targets lacking either class are unpaired. Violins use bounded Scott-bandwidth KDE in log10 CSP space with equal area for the displayed truncated densities; dots and thick bars show medians and interquartile ranges. Groups with <5 observations or zero variance show observations only. Axes retain ppm labels on logarithmic scales. All intervals are pointwise/scalar percentile 95% intervals from 2000 whole-target bootstrap replicates; related receptor families may remain correlated. Reconstructed m=1 and saved significance disagree for 1 rounded CSP boundary observation(s), exported in residue_eligibility.csv. AUC measures binding-site localization; distal CSPs can be biologically meaningful. The companion atlas includes every target.
