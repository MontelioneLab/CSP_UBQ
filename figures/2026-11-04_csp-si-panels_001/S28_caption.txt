SI Fig. S28. Magnitudes of significant HN chemical-shift perturbations within and outside the binding site. Distributions pool significant CSPs across the same 135 pH/temperature-matched targets and eligibility rules used in SI Fig. S27: 1,331 binding-site residues (TP, green) and 3,023 distal residues (FP, purple). Group membership uses the saved primary significance masks, corresponding to CSP >= max(cleaned mean CSP, 0.05 ppm) for each target. These distributions contain only significant CSPs. Magnitudes are in ppm on a logarithmic axis. Violins show Scott-bandwidth kernel density estimates in log10 CSP space, bounded by the observed ranges and normalized to equal area. White dots show medians; thick black lines show interquartile ranges. Each residue has equal weight in the pooled distributions; violin width represents density. TP and FP refer to the structural binding-site definition (Methods); distal CSPs can reflect real biological responses.
