SI Fig. S27. Receiver operating characteristic analysis of CSP magnitude and binding-site membership. ROC curves for 135 pH/temperature-matched apo/holo targets (12,267 eligible residues) were obtained by varying the HN CSP significance threshold while keeping structural binding-site labels and residue eligibility fixed. Gray lines show individual targets. The blue curve pools all eligible residues using CSP divided by the target-specific primary cutoff, max(cleaned mean CSP, 0.05 ppm). The black dashed curve is the equal-target mean ROC on a common false-positive-rate grid; mean AUC is the arithmetic mean of the 135 individual target AUCs. Pooled AUC is 0.704 (95% CI 0.682-0.728); mean target AUC is 0.728 (95% CI 0.707-0.750). The shaded band is the pointwise 95% interval for the mean ROC; intervals use 2,000 whole-target bootstrap replicates. Related receptor families may remain correlated. The gray diagonal indicates chance ranking; the star marks the pooled operating point from saved primary significance masks. Residues lacking valid CSPs or structural labels and the extreme CSP-z outlier (z > 100) are excluded.

SI Fig. S28. Magnitudes of significant HN chemical-shift perturbations within and outside the binding site. Distributions pool significant CSPs across the same 135 pH/temperature-matched targets and eligibility rules used in SI Fig. S27: 1,331 binding-site residues (TP, green) and 3,023 distal residues (FP, purple). Group membership uses the saved primary significance masks, corresponding to CSP >= max(cleaned mean CSP, 0.05 ppm) for each target. These distributions contain only significant CSPs. Magnitudes are in ppm on a logarithmic axis. Violins show Scott-bandwidth kernel density estimates in log10 CSP space, bounded by the observed ranges and normalized to equal area. White dots show medians; thick black lines show interquartile ranges. Each residue has equal weight in the pooled distributions; violin width represents density. TP and FP refer to the structural binding-site definition (Methods); distal CSPs can reflect real biological responses.
