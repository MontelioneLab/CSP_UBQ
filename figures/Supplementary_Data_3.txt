Supplementary Data 3. Per-residue CSPs for Figure 2, row B.

This table is the per-residue data for Figure 2B (holo PDB 2M14, apo BMRB 6225, holo BMRB 18842). Each row is one holo receptor residue that has a recorded HN CSP and a TP, FP, TN, or FN label. Those values are the CSP classification bar in the first column of the row. CSP Mask, Binding Site Mask, and CSP Classification are the residue sets drawn on the three structure panels (CSP mask, binding site, and TP/FP/TN/FN).

Columns:
- Residue: holo sequence number.
- CSP magnitude: combined HN chemical-shift perturbation (ppm).
- CSP Mask: True when the CSP is significant under the primary cutoff.
- Binding Site Mask: True when the residue is in the binding-site union.
- CSP Classification: TP (significant CSP in the binding site), FP (significant CSP outside the binding site), TN (low CSP outside the binding site), or FN (low CSP in the binding site).
